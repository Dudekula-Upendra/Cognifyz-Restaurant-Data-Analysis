# 🍽️ Restaurant Data Analysis | Cognifyz Technologies Internship

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Data Analyst Internship Program at Cognifyz Technologies**. The objective was to perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) on a restaurant dataset and generate meaningful business insights using Python and data visualization techniques.

The project covers multiple levels of analysis, including cuisine trends, city-wise restaurant performance, pricing patterns, online delivery services, customer ratings, geographic analysis, and restaurant chain performance.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Folium
- GeoPandas
- Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

The dataset contains information about restaurants, including:

- Restaurant Name
- City
- Cuisine Type
- Aggregate Rating
- Votes
- Price Range
- Online Delivery Availability
- Geographic Coordinates (Latitude & Longitude)

---

# 📊 Level 1: Basic Data Analysis

## 1️⃣ Top Cuisines Analysis

### Objectives
- Identify the top three most common cuisines.
- Calculate the percentage of restaurants serving each cuisine.

### Key Outcome
- Identified the most popular cuisines and market demand trends.

---

## 2️⃣ City Analysis

### Objectives
- Identify the city with the highest number of restaurants.
- Calculate average restaurant ratings by city.
- Determine the city with the highest average rating.

### Key Outcome
- Gained insights into regional restaurant performance.

---

## 3️⃣ Price Range Distribution

### Objectives
- Visualize restaurant price ranges using charts.
- Calculate the percentage of restaurants in each price category.

### Key Outcome
- Better understanding of customer affordability segments.

---

## 4️⃣ Online Delivery Analysis

### Objectives
- Determine the percentage of restaurants offering online delivery.
- Compare ratings of restaurants with and without online delivery.

### Key Outcome
- Evaluated the impact of delivery services on customer satisfaction.

---

# 📈 Level 2: Advanced Data Analysis

## 1️⃣ Restaurant Ratings Analysis

### Objectives
- Analyze the distribution of aggregate ratings.
- Determine the most common rating range.
- Calculate average votes received by restaurants.

### Key Outcome
- Identified customer rating patterns and engagement levels.

---

## 2️⃣ Cuisine Combination Analysis

### Objectives
- Identify the most common cuisine combinations.
- Analyze whether certain combinations receive higher ratings.

### Key Outcome
- Discovered customer preferences for cuisine pairings.

---

## 3️⃣ Geographic Analysis

### Objectives
- Plot restaurant locations using latitude and longitude.
- Identify geographic clusters and concentration areas.

### Key Outcome
- Revealed location-based restaurant trends and opportunities.

---

## 4️⃣ Restaurant Chain Analysis

### Objectives
- Identify restaurant chains present in the dataset.
- Analyze ratings and popularity of different chains.

### Key Outcome
- Evaluated brand performance and customer loyalty.
